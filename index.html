The Problem

Why existing tools fail

Enterprise communication today is broken. Here's what happens every single day.

📧

The Email Graveyard

Important surveys and policy acknowledgments get buried under hundreds of unread emails. Average open rate for internal emails: 21%.

💬

Slack & Teams Noise

Critical announcements drown in a river of GIFs, threads, and casual conversations. Messages scroll out of sight within minutes.

🔀

Context Switching Tax

Employees must leave their workflow, open another app, navigate to the right channel, find the message, and respond. Most never do.

📊

Zero Accountability

Leadership has no way to verify who read a critical security advisory, who acknowledged a policy change, or whether quorum was reached.

The Solution

What is Ribbit?

Ribbit is an enterprise signal platform that delivers polls, priority alerts, and multi-section forms directly to employee desktops — without requiring them to search inboxes, monitor busy chat channels, or switch contexts.

01

Publisher Web Studio

Leaders author, target, schedule, and analyze signals through a guided 4-step wizard.

02

Consumer Desktop App

Employees receive clean, elegant cards in the corner of their screen — meeting-aware, quiet during calls, and respectful of focus.

03

Guaranteed Response

Automatic polite escalation ensures critical communications and surveys reach completion without manual chasing.

Web Studio & Desktop Client
Consumer Desktop App

Context-aware. Respectful. Impossible to miss.

The desktop client adapts its behavior to the employee's current state — so it's never annoying, but always effective.

Normal Desktop Focus

A compact, elegant card appears in the corner of the screen. Employee responds with 1 click — no context switch needed.

In a Meeting or Screen Sharing

The signal is silently held. No popup interrupts a customer call or board presentation. Signals appear the moment the meeting ends.

Remind Me Later

Employee snoozes the signal for 10m, 30m, 1h, or a custom time. The card contracts into a tiny reminder pill and re-expands at the chosen time.

Workstation Locked / Asleep

Signals wait safely while the computer is locked. When the employee unlocks their screen, any missed signals appear immediately so nothing is lost.

Offline / No Network

Employees can respond even when disconnected or traveling. Responses save safely and sync automatically the moment connection returns.

Signal Escalation

Three escalation tiers, one intelligent system

Ribbit automatically escalates signals from gentle to assertive based on urgency and approaching deadlines.

Tier 1 Desktop Card

A compact, elegant notification card in the corner of the screen. Non-intrusive. Easy to respond in 1 click or snooze with "Remind Me Later" without breaking workflow.

When: Default polite delivery for all standard quick polls, announcements, and forms.
Tier 2 Instant Notification Modal

A dedicated signal type delivered as an interactive notification modal on screen. Employees can read and answer directly inside the modal without opening another app or switching contexts.

Signal Type: Instant Modal Poll, Instant Modal Alert, Instant Modal Form — immediate in-place answering.
Tier 3 Persistent Lockdown

The definitive OS-level lockdown mode. Pinned to the screen edge always-on-top with a live countdown timer. It stays locked on screen across all workspaces and blocks dismissal or app quitting until the employee answers.

Lockdown Trigger: Approaching expiration deadline (configurable lead buffer) or publisher-enforced persistent compliance.
